## 🌟 Overview
**Bounz** is an addictive, fast-paced Endless Runner where timing is everything. Charge your jump, leap across platforms, collect coins, and avoid deadly traps! Designed from the ground up for mobile devices with a seamless, satisfying single-tap control scheme.

## ✨ Key Features
- **👆 One-Tap Controls:** Press to charge, release to jump! The longer you hold, the further you fly.
- **🎯 Perfect Landing Combo:** Land dead-center on platforms to build your combo multiplier and earn massive scores.
- **🛡️ Power-Ups & Hazards:** Utilize Shields, Magnets, and Score Multipliers to survive moving enemies, spikes, and fragile platforms.
- **🛍️ Skin Store:** Collect coins to unlock and equip unique cosmetic skins for your character.
- **📜 Daily Quests:** Complete dynamic missions (e.g., "Collect 50 Coins") to earn rewards and keep the gameplay fresh.
- **🌅 Dynamic Themes:** Watch the game's atmosphere seamlessly transition from day to night as your score increases.
- **🔊 Rich Audio & Haptics:** Dynamic pitch-scaling for combos and built-in mobile vibration feedback.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack
- **Engine:** Unity 2D (C#)
- **Architecture:** Event-Driven (`GameEvents` Action bus), Object Pooling for performance, ScriptableObjects for configuration.
- **Clean Code:** 100% comment-free, production-ready C# codebase.
- **Platform:** Optimized for Mobile (iOS & Android).

## 🚀 Getting Started
1. Clone this repository:
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/Bor-Code/Bounz.git
   ```
2. Open the project in **Unity**.
3. Open the main scene and hit **Play**!

## 🎮 How to Play
1. **Hold** the screen to charge your jump.
2. **Release** at the right moment to leap to the next platform.
3. Don't fall, avoid the moving spikes, and grab as many coins as you can!
